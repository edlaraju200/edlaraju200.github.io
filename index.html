About

IAM Engineer & CIAM Specialist

I design and operate enterprise Identity & Access Management platforms for regulated financial institutions, with deep expertise in IBM Security Access Manager (ISAM/WebSEAL), Okta Auth0, and PingFederate.

My focus is CIAM modernization — migrating legacy on-premise authentication infrastructure to cloud-native identity platforms, while maintaining enterprise-grade security, compliance, and availability across hybrid environments.

I specialize in SAML 2.0, OAuth 2.0, and OIDC federation protocols, WebSEAL junction management, SSO architecture, and identity lifecycle automation using JavaScript mapping rules and PowerShell scripting.
